Dock mode

Starts by itself

Charging, auto-rotate on, phone set on its side for 2 seconds. Picked up or unplugged, it closes.

Alarms only

Do Not Disturb switches to alarms only while docked, and your old setting comes back after.

On the lock screen

Pick Glint Dock as the screen saver once. Android then shows it whenever the phone charges.

Kind to the screen

Dim red at night, and the whole layout shifts a few pixels each minute so nothing burns in.

Wallpapers

Eight palettes in three patterns. The phone draws each one at its exact screen size, so they're always sharp and add nothing to the download.
